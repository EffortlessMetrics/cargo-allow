# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the `cargo-allow` project a welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We act in the interest of a healthy open source community and prioritize clear,
kind, and constructive collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences, and constraints.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and process rather than on
  people.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Acknowledging mistakes and working to repair harm when it occurs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, demeaning comments, personal attacks, or derogatory jokes.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or unwanted
  physical contact.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate misrepresentation, or bad-faith participation.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports or helps address a concern.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional community setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, chat or mailing-list spaces, documentation, examples,
community events, and any other venue where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project
maintainers at conduct@effortlessmetrics.dev.

Reports should include, when possible:

- A description of what happened.
- Links, screenshots, timestamps, or other relevant context.
- The names or handles of people involved.
- Any immediate safety or privacy concerns.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters and affected people
as much as possible while reviewing and responding to the report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying expectations and may take any action
they deem appropriate and proportionate, including:

1. Private clarification or warning.
2. Requesting edits, retractions, or apologies.
3. Temporary moderation or restricted participation.
4. Removal from project spaces.
5. Permanent ban from project participation.

Maintainers who are involved in a report should recuse themselves from handling
that report when possible. Retaliation for reporting or participating in an
enforcement process is a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by common open source community standards and
adapted for the `cargo-allow` project.
